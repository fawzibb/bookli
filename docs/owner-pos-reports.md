# Owner reports on the Bookli website

Reports are available from the owner dashboard card and sidebar for a business that has activated POS at least once. The page is owner-only, tenant-scoped and private (including CSV). An expired POS subscription preserves read access to already synchronized reports; upload pauses until POS renewal. Website subscription expiry does not remove POS report history.

Reports mirror every active product, including unpublished products; physical/reserved/available stock; weighted average cost and inventory value; sales by item/category/payment/cashier; gross profit; refunds/cancellations; stock and cash movements; and existing historical/synchronization review issues. Date filters use the originating POS timezone. Current inventory is a current snapshot, not reconstructed historical stock. Sales include customer-account invoices and are distinct from cash flow. Refunds and amendments remain attributed to the original selling cashier, while reversal details also show the processing cashier.

The signed-in licensed primary automatically sends reports independently of website catalog publication. It creates a consistent SQLite backup, then reads that temporary copy so a long history scan does not hold a checkout lock. Only curated report fields are exported; no passwords, PINs, device tokens, customer contact details, subscription secrets or signing keys are exported. Reports never modify local inventory, cash or invoices.

Uploads use a durable source UUID and monotonic version, SHA-256 manifest/chunk checks, and transactional begin/chunk/complete endpoints. A new snapshot becomes visible only after every chunk and dataset count is verified. Old complete data remains available during interruption. Retries reuse retained bytes and identifiers. Missing/expired staging restarts safely; a changed database identity or older restored version cannot silently replace newer history. Unchanged snapshots send a heartbeat about once per minute. The page shows data-capture time, last server contact and stale/expired notices. First upload and large histories can take several synchronization cycles.

Monetary values and quantities are decimal strings. Website aggregation/rounding uses exact decimal arithmetic without floats or optional PHP extensions. CSV escapes spreadsheet formulas and exports every matching row; HTML pages display 50 rows and printing covers the current page. Arabic and English are supported. Historical cost/ledger flags are retained for review rather than guessed or automatically corrected.

Deploy only the additive `2026_11_09_000001_add_pos_owner_reports.php` migration; do not reset existing schemas or copy the test environment. Current transfer limits: 128 MB of serialized data per snapshot, 2,000,000 total rows, at most 500 rows per chunk, and 2,000 item lines per financial event. Exceeding limits retains the previous website report and shows a sync error; it does not stop POS sales. LAN/internal shop server work remains deferred.
